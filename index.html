Our story

Not your regular popcorn.
This one has a mission.

Poptein® started with a simple thought: why does protein taste like punishment?

Somewhere along the way it became chalky shakes and snacks you eat just for the macros. We weren't into that. Protein isn't a side quest, so it should taste like something you actually crave.

Now popping across 110+ stores, HORECA and fitness centres — gym bags, movie nights and everyday cravings.
